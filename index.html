Solvexo

Solvexo is an all-in-one commerce platform for sellers, creators and educators. With Solvexo you can build your own online store, sell in person with Solvexo POS, and manage products, inventory, orders, customers, payments, marketing and analytics in one place.

How Solvexo uses Google sign-in

Sellers can create and sign in to their Solvexo account with Google. Solvexo only receives your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them solely to create your account and sign you in. Store owners can also connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics with read-only access to see their own search and visitor reports inside Solvexo. Google user data is never sold or used for advertising.
